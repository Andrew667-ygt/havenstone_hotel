Policies & FAQ

Everything you need to know before your stay

Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out can be arranged upon request, subject to availability. Please contact the front desk for arrangements.

Free cancellation is available up to 48 hours before your scheduled check-in date. Cancellations made within 48 hours will be charged one night's stay. No-shows will be charged the full booking amount.

Yes! Havenstone Hotel is pet-friendly. We welcome dogs and cats weighing up to 25 kg. A pet fee of $30 per night applies. Pet beds and bowls are provided upon request.

A complimentary gourmet buffet breakfast is included with all room types. Breakfast is served daily from 6:30 AM to 10:30 AM in our main dining hall. Room service breakfast is also available at an additional charge.

Yes, we offer a complimentary airport shuttle for guests staying 3 nights or more. For shorter stays, our shuttle service is available at $25 per trip. Please book at least 24 hours in advance.

Complimentary on-site parking is available for all hotel guests. We also offer valet parking for $15 per day. Electric vehicle charging stations are available in the parking area.

Absolutely. You can modify your dates, room type, or guest count at no extra charge up to 48 hours before check-in. Changes within 48 hours are subject to availability and may incur a rate difference.

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex), mobile payments (Apple Pay, Google Pay), and bank transfers. A valid card is required at the time of booking for guarantee.
